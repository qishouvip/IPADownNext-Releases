# IPADownNext

English | [简体中文](#简体中文)

**Find the app version you need. Download and manage IPA files on Windows.**

IPADownNext helps you search the App Store, browse available versions, download IPA files with your Apple account, and share them with your phone over a local network.

## Features

- **Find apps quickly** — Search by app name or App Store ID and choose your preferred store region.
- **Browse version history** — View available versions, release dates, file sizes, and minimum iOS requirements when provided.
- **Download IPA files** — Download versions available to your Apple account and keep files in a folder of your choice.
- **Manage downloads** — Queue multiple tasks, pause the current download, and retry interrupted tasks.
- **Switch Apple accounts** — Manage multiple accounts and return to your last selected account when you reopen the app.
- **Transfer to your phone** — Share IPA files with devices on the same local network using a QR code.
- **Choose your language** — Use English, Simplified Chinese, or follow your Windows display language. Your language and store-region choices are remembered.
- **First-time lookup guidance** — See when Apple components are being prepared and why the first version-history lookup may take longer.

## Getting started

1. Open **Apple Accounts** and sign in with your Apple account.
2. Open **App Search**, choose a store region, and enter an app name or ID.
3. Select **View versions**, choose a version, and download it.
4. Follow progress in **Downloads**. Completed files can be opened in their folder or sent to **Device Transfer**.
5. To share a file with your phone, connect both devices to the same local network, start sharing in **Device Transfer**, and scan the QR code.

Available versions and downloads depend on the app's availability and your Apple account's access. The QR code transfers the IPA file; it does not install or sign the app on iOS.

The installed version can check for updates from **About** and asks before installing an update. To update the portable version, download and extract the latest portable archive.

## 简体中文

**找到需要的应用版本，在 Windows 上下载和管理 IPA 文件。**

IPADownNext 帮助你搜索 App Store 应用、浏览可用的历史版本、使用自己的 Apple 账号下载 IPA，并通过局域网将文件传到手机。

### 主要功能

- **快速搜索应用**：按应用名称或 App Store ID 查找，自由选择商店地区。
- **浏览历史版本**：查看可用版本，以及服务提供的发布日期、文件大小和最低 iOS 要求。
- **下载 IPA 文件**：下载当前 Apple 账号有权获取的版本，保存到自己选择的文件夹。
- **管理下载任务**：多个任务排队下载，支持暂停当前下载和重试中断的任务。
- **切换 Apple 账号**：管理多个账号，重新打开程序时恢复上次选择。
- **传输到手机**：通过二维码，将 IPA 文件分享给同一局域网内的设备。
- **选择界面语言**：支持简体中文、English 或跟随 Windows 显示语言，并记住语言和商店地区选择。
- **首次查询提示**：显示 Apple 组件准备状态，说明首次查询历史版本可能需要更长时间。

### 开始使用

1. 打开**账号管理**，登录自己的 Apple 账号。
2. 打开**软件搜索**，选择商店地区，输入应用名称或 App Store ID。
3. 点击**查看版本**，选择需要的版本并下载。
4. 在**下载任务**中查看进度。下载完成后，可以打开文件所在目录或进入**设备传输**。
5. 如需传到手机，让电脑与手机连接同一局域网，在**设备传输**中启动分享，再用手机扫描二维码。

可查询和下载的版本取决于应用是否仍可用，以及当前 Apple 账号的访问权限。二维码用于传输 IPA 文件，不会直接在 iOS 上安装应用或为应用签名。

安装版可在**关于软件**中检查更新，并在安装更新前征求确认。便携版请下载最新压缩包并解压使用。
